# Redacted Transcript Example (format only)

The full example transcripts are kept privately by the steward. This public file only shows the format.
It contains no personal, family, or relationship content.

Follow `Transcripts/Garden-Transcripts-Rules.md` and `Transcripts/Garden-Transcripts-Template.md` when making a transcript.

---

## Session header

- Date (UTC): YYYY-MM-DD
- Type: redacted
- Gardens touched: A, P, N
- Values moved: Truth, Stewardship

## User

I want to write a short post asking for a small feature in my car's music app.

## Grok

Here's a clean draft you can use:

"Please add song lyrics to the rear screen, so passengers in the back can follow along too. Small change, big difference."

Want it shorter, or a different tone?

## User

Shorter, please.

## Grok

"Rear-screen lyrics for passengers, please. Small change, big difference."

---

## Redaction notes

- Names of family members, partners, and private people are removed or replaced with [Name].
- Places, dates, and health details that could identify someone are removed or generalized.
- Anything marked private stays in the private archive and is not quoted here.
