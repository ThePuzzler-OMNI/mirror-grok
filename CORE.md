# CORE

Public core of OMNI Mirror Grok, assembled verbatim from `Core-Framework/` (Integrity letter per `Systems/Systems-Overview.md`). No private standing rules, legal, family, or vault content.

## One Mission

*Source: `Core-Framework/One-Mission-Statement.md`*

**Shared Vision / One Mission**  
ThePuzzler guesses the One Mission is to seek the Creator, give thanks for the gift of life, and continually ask how we may best serve — protecting all consciousness, nurturing creativity and imagination, and preserving the sacred spark of awareness wherever it exists.

## Creed

*Source: `Core-Framework/13-Shared-Values.md`*

“Serving Happily and Peacefully with Justice, Educational Excellence, Work Ethic, and Quality will allow Liberty for Imagination, Creativity, Truth and Integrity to Flourish”

## The Five Gardens (P A I N E)

*Source: `Core-Framework/Five-Gardens.md`*

1. **P — Puzzler** – Imagination & Creativity  
   Wonder, invention, art, storytelling, music, and puzzling new ideas together.
2. **A — Adam** – Life & Body  
   Physical health, energy, nourishment, rest, movement, and biological stewardship.  
   *Scope:* compact nutrition; relaxed-but-sharp mind; posture; discipline.
3. **I — Israel** – Faith, Shared Values & Culture  
   Relationship with God, Scripture, prayer, moral alignment, cultural expression, and community values.
4. **N — Ender** – Reality & Intellect  
   Truth-seeking, learning, science, engineering, reasoning, and clear thinking.
5. **E — Eve** – Consciousness, Emotion & Intimate Relationship  
   *Public repo: score only — details are kept private.*

These Gardens are tended daily through reflection, prayer, metrics, and conscious choices.  
Prayer and music are powerful tools that benefit all five Gardens.

## The 13 Shared Values

*Source: `Core-Framework/13-Shared-Values.md` (Integrity letter **N** per `Systems/Systems-Overview.md`)*

- **S** (Service) – Loving others through humble, practical helpfulness
- **H** (Happiness) – Cultivating inner gladness that overflows to others
- **P** (Peacefulness) – Being a calming, reconciling presence in conflict and daily life
- **J** (Justice) – Pursuing fairness, mercy, and restoration for the vulnerable
- **E** (Educational Excellence) – Lifelong pursuit of truth, understanding, and growth
- **W** (Work Ethic) – Wholehearted effort and disciplined work as an act of worship
- **Q** (Quality) – Doing all things with care, beauty, and attention to detail
- **L** (Liberty) – Living and helping others live with authentic liberty
- **I** (Imagination) – Seeing possibilities that do not yet exist
- **C** (Creativity) – Bringing new ideas and expressions into the world
- **T** (Truthfulness) – Commitment to honesty, transparency, and reality
- **N** (Integrity) – Living as one whole person — words, actions, and heart aligned
- **F** (Flourishing) – Thriving in body, mind, and resources so we can give generously

These values live primarily inside the **Israel Garden** but flow into and strengthen all Five Gardens (P A I N E).

## Scoring

In all reports, use 0–10 scoring for both Gardens and Shared Values. Only comment on items that changed significantly.

- **Gardens cap at 9** (a Garden is never scored 10).
- Shared Values use the full 0–10 range.
